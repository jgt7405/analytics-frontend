# Wins Box-and-Whisker Modernization Concepts

These five concept infographics were generated from `current-wins-page.png` as a visual reference. They preserve the existing vertical box-and-whisker chart, team-logo baseline, light theme, and JThom Analytics palette while borrowing the softer surfaces and visual hierarchy of the neighboring win-distribution tables.

## Final prompt set

### 1. Soft Percentile Capsules

Modernize the familiar box plot with slender rounded 25th–75th percentile capsules, soft translucent team-color fills, 1.5 px outlines, hairline whiskers, circular end dots, a strong median notch, faint horizontal grid lines, generous whitespace, and a softly elevated white chart card. Annotate: “Softer IQR,” “Cleaner whiskers,” and “Stronger median.”

### 2. Layered Confidence Bands

Use muted hairline full-range whiskers, narrow semi-transparent 25th–75th percentile bands with softly rounded corners, a solid diamond for the median, a small hollow ring for the average, subtle alternating plot bands, and only four major grid lines. Annotate: “Layered range,” “Median diamond,” and “Quieter grid.”

### 3. Team Lanes

Place every team in a faint vertical lane aligned to its logo. Use an ultra-thin whisker, small rounded cap ticks, a compact soft-filled IQR block, and a crisp median-value pill. Highlight one lane with a light hover state and a tooltip containing median, middle-50-percent, and likely-range values. Annotate: “Guided lanes,” “Value label,” and “Focus state.”

### 4. Floating IQR Tiles

Render the 25th–75th percentile as a compact floating tile with 8 px radius, subtle team-tinted fill, thin team-color top accent, and restrained shadow. Put neutral slate whiskers behind the tile, show a short dark median line, reveal low/high labels only for the focused team, and place logos in circular baseline wells. Annotate: “Floating tiles,” “Quiet whiskers,” and “Logo baseline.”

### 5. Comparison Focus

Keep most distributions in soft neutral slate with rounded whiskers and translucent IQR capsules. Highlight two compared teams in team color, add a subtle vertical focus glow, show median chips only for those teams, and include a compact comparison ribbon and median-delta annotation. Annotate: “Calm default,” “Compare focus,” and “Useful delta.”

## Shared visual constraints

- Premium, airy sports-analytics UI with restrained JThom teal accents
- White and cool-gray surfaces, thin borders, compact typography, and subtle elevation
- Correct vertical box-and-whisker semantics and a team-emblem baseline
- No heavy black strokes, dense grid, excessive gradients, photos, or unrelated UI
